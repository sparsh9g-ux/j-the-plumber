# J the Plumber

A work-in-progress demo site built for a potential prospect being landed through Spark Automation, our AI automation agency for local service businesses.

## How this was built

Sparsh picked the prospect, set the direction — a clean, trustworthy single-page site in a blue/cyan/yellow color scheme — and decided what to lead with: licensed & insured, 24/7 emergency availability, and a quote form that captures leads even when the plumber's on a job. Pricing ($300 setup, $75/month) was his call too.

This was built in close collaboration with Claude — back-and-forth brainstorming on layout and copy, working through revisions together. Before it went out for the actual sales call, the live Vercel deploy was checked and reviewed against what would actually land in the room, not just handed over as-built.
